**Gridiron Tactics** is a two-minute lane game. You and the opponent each send a deck of eight players up a 100-yard field. First to two touchdowns wins. If the clock hits 0:00, the higher score wins, and a tied score is a draw.

## The field

You play from the bottom. Your goal line is yard **0**. The opponent’s goal line is yard **100**. The ball starts on the **50**, in the center lane.

There are five lanes. One player can stand on a spot. Anyone stacked behind a teammate waits, then steps forward when the spot opens.

## A card

The circled number is the elixir cost. The two numbers under the name are strength and speed.

| Speed | Time to move one tile |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 second |
| 2 | 0.75 seconds |
| 3 | 0.5 seconds |

Strength is how long a player lasts in a fight. The bar over a token is that same number. A gold ring around a token means those two are fighting right now.

## Before the snap

Pick eight different cards. On the right-hand list, click a row to send that card to the back of the deck. The top of that list is what you will draw first. **START** turns green when the deck has eight cards.

## During the match

You begin with **5** elixir. It refills by 1 every 2 seconds, up to **10**.

Your hand shows four cards. The **NEXT** card is the one waiting behind them. Click a card, then click a lane. The player appears on your yard 0 in that lane, and the card goes to the back of the deck. If that goal-line spot is already full, the card stays in your hand and the elixir stays unspent.

The opponent does the same from the top goal line, and prefers the lane the ball is in.

## The ball

Anyone except a Bulldozer picks the ball up by standing on it, as long as they are free to move. Carrying the ball onto the opponent’s goal line scores, in any lane.

A player who reaches that goal line without the ball is stuck there and loses **3** strength per second.

## Fights

Two enemies fight when they share a spot, or when one stands on the next tile straight ahead of the other. Each loses **1** strength per second. The fight ends when one reaches 0 and leaves the field. The survivor stays put. The next player in line walks into the empty spot and the fight can start again.

Strength only falls in a fight, on the goal line, or from a character ability. Standing still does nothing to it.

## Momentum

Every two tiles a player runs without being in a fight, they gain one momentum. Some characters spend that momentum on a lane change or on extra strength. Starting a fight clears the “since the last fight” count. A Guard keeps the lifetime count.

## Passing and kicking

**PASS** costs **2** elixir. It lights up when your ball-carrier has someone to throw to and you can pay.

The gold oval is the throw’s reach. It grows and shrinks by one tile about as often as that carrier takes a step, and it stays at least 2 tiles wide. A teammate has to be half a tile inside the oval. Passer, Sidewinder, Pinball, and Blitz also add their momentum to that reach. The other characters use strength alone.

The arrow shows the chosen target:

- A solid gold arrow is a throw toward your own forward half.
- A dashed blue arrow is a throw behind the carrier.
- A red arrow means an enemy is standing one tile straight ahead, and that enemy will catch the ball.

The game prefers a teammate in the forward half, and the one closest to straight ahead. With nobody there, it throws backward, and a tie goes to the lane closer to the left sideline. A Bulldozer is never a receiver.

From the **center lane**, the opponent’s goal post can be the target. Hitting it scores. Your own post is never a target. A kick from a side lane does not score.

## After a score

The first touchdown leaves everyone on the field. Both elixir bars fill to 10. The ball is gone for one second, then a new ball appears on the 50 in the center lane. Anyone already standing there picks it up. The second touchdown ends the match.

## Offense

**Passer** — cost 1, strength 2, speed 2. A cheap body and a thrower. Momentum widens the pass ring.

**Sidewinder** — cost 2, strength 3, speed 2. On every third momentum (about every six free tiles), it steps one lane toward the left sideline. From the left sideline, that step goes inward instead.

**B-line** — cost 2, strength 1, speed 3. Very fast and fragile at the snap. Every third momentum since the last fight, it gains 1 strength. A long open run can turn it into a strength-4 player. A fight resets that buildup.

**Bulldozer** — cost 6, strength 6, speed 1. It does not carry the ball. When it meets someone, it shoves them backward one tile for each point of strength it has over them, until a body or the edge stops the shove. If the shove cannot move them even one tile, the Bulldozer dies and that opponent burns for 6 strength over the next second.

**Pinball** — cost 3, strength 2, speed 3. Every momentum (every two free tiles) it hops one lane sideways. It starts toward the left sideline and reverses at either sideline. Momentum also widens its pass ring.

**Blitz** — cost 2, strength 1, speed 3. Momentum widens its pass ring. If it has run more than two tiles and a fight starts while it has the ball, it drops the ball one tile behind itself.

## Defense

**Guard** — cost 4, strength 4, speed 2. Every other momentum, for its whole life, it gains 1 strength. A fight does not wipe that progress, so a Guard that has been running gets harder to move.

**Tripwire** — cost 2, strength 2, speed 2. At the start of a fight, if the opponent has the ball and more strength, Tripwire knocks the ball loose one tile behind itself. Otherwise it strips 1 strength from that opponent.

**Interceptor** — cost 3, strength 2, speed 3. A fast defender. Any enemy one tile straight ahead of a throw catches it. This card is the body you put in that spot.

**Sweeper** — cost 5, strength 4, speed 1. The first time it reaches the 50 while it is free to move, it slides sideways toward the lane with the most offensive players. It can cross up to as many lanes as its momentum, and it stops early if the next spot is full. It does this once.
